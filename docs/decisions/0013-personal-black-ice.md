# 0013 — Personal black-ice exhibits and resume finale

Status: accepted and implemented locally. Date: 2026-10-25. Refines [0012](0012-underwater-portfolio.md); engine, reference direction and branch/deployment boundaries remain unchanged.

## Context

The owner requested black ice, personal sports objects, introduction/experience/projects in that order, stronger reference-like interaction and atmosphere, minimal typed popups, a slower multilingual entrance, and a complete-page resume as the final composition. The previous limited processor rocking and fish translation were visibly insufficient.

## Decision

Use original procedural basketball, boxing-glove and dumbbell artwork inside the three physical ice shells. Preserve the old sculptures as dormant attributed assets. Give hover tilt, drag accumulation and camera parallax separate damped channels. A low-resolution advected cursor field creates the rising aurora response; marine snow and occasional shooting stars share the scene clock.

The separate particle processor has a thick package, raised die, rear contacts and four pin banks, with continuous .05rad/s Y rotation and local-space cursor/fish response. A scroll-driven fish entrance feeds a continuous swimming circuit with heading and tail deformation, then exits before the resume settles. Pause/reduced motion freeze nonessential time and preserve navigation.

Keep native modal semantics but simplify their initial presentation to a heading, typed paragraph and compact topic selectors. Screen readers receive complete copy immediately. Expandable full records preserve all experience, projects, metrics and education.

Render the exact public one-page resume to a high-resolution PNG for the homepage and reading finale; fit the whole page without a nested PDF scroller. Preserve the actual PDF at its existing route with download/open actions. Display email, GitHub and LinkedIn directly. A four-second greeting cycle precedes the existing camera approach; reduced motion skips these animations.

## Consequences

No new runtime dependencies or third-party sports assets are required. The new preview must be regenerated when the source resume PDF changes. Two small render targets add cursor persistence without a full-resolution simulation. Fish motion and the processor remain independently authored approximations of the measured reference; they are not transplanted source code.
