# 0015 — Nightfall: contact-responsive black ice and atmospheric descent

Status: accepted and implemented locally. Date: 2026-10-28. Supersedes the physical systems-chamber art direction and basic sound design in [0014](0014-systems-chamber-and-adaptive-graphics.md); retains its adaptive graphics and scroll-ordered halos.

## Owner direction

The owner rejected pale frost and the manufactured processor pedestal. They requested a closer hybrid of the existing gallery and Igloo’s surface interactions, geometric effects and layered sounds, with an original theme. They requested a scroll-linked descent from the third exhibit through the processor toward an upper-atmosphere sky, and straightforward access for recruiters. Local review only; do not push or deploy.

## Decision

Use an original **Nightfall** identity: charcoal-black mineral ice, restrained cold reflections, subtle contact geometry, and a gradual blue-hour atmospheric horizon. Keep the personal basketball, gloves and dumbbell, all factual records, the volumetric processor identity, and the complete resume finale.

Extend Three’s physical material rather than replace the renderer. Object-space strata, fracture roughness and micro-relief establish the surface. A bounded, decaying cursor-contact history locally polishes the material, clears its refraction tint, and exposes a thin crystalline frontier. Contact moves with the rotating object and freezes with pause/reduced motion. Use fewer contacts and no physical transmission in cheaper profiles.

Sparse points and short connections occupy each artifact’s local space and respond to proximity. Keep projected DOM hotspots sharp and follow scroll order. Replace the physical processor stage with an assembling point volume and restrained field marks. Scroll erodes the third shell and gathers its particle field into the processor; the later field disperses while the sky gradually develops a horizon and cloud deck. Every transition derives from normalized progress and reverses consistently.

Use original buffered Web Audio: suspended harmonic ambience, movement-linked travel, glass contact, varied hover taps, text ticks, open/close, transition and arrival. Audio remains explicit opt-in with shared reverb, bounded voices, gain limiting, hidden-tab suspension and complete cleanup. No reference recording is licensed for redistribution by its public availability.

Recruiter access takes priority over mystery navigation. Direct About, Projects, Experience and Resume actions sit in the header; a labeled five-stop journey rail permits predictable forward/back travel. Replace the fictitious meter-depth readout with section names. Remove shooting meteors; retain restrained depth particles and cursor atmosphere. Keep reading mode, keyboard access, no-script/WebGL fallback and content routes.

## Evidence and limits

[Igloo study](../reports/igloo-hybrid-study.md) distinguishes rendered observations, mechanisms verified in its public client, and original adaptations. Its authored recordings have no verified permissive license, so none are bundled or hotlinked. This implementation studies behavior and composition, not proprietary source or assets. No new runtime dependency or framework rewrite is needed. The result does not claim pixel identity or universal frame rates.

## Artifact material fidelity refinement — 2026-10-28

The owner requested Igloo’s rock shape, texture, shader appearance and opaqueness while keeping black ice. Match its chipped slab silhouettes, rough mottled frost, broken ledges and readable refractive interiors. Replace the clean convex gemstone hull and broad smooth specular strips. Dark outer reflectance and relatively neutral transmitted light are separate channels; a uniformly black refraction tint obscures the personal objects. The implementation remains independently authored with existing Three physical lighting/contact APIs. Local-only review, content and adaptive rendering remain unchanged.
