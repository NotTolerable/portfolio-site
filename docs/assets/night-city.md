# Night-city artwork

Created for this portfolio on 2026-10-23 and integrated during the 2026-10-24 rebrand.

- Final project asset: [`public/illustrations/night-city.webp`](../../public/illustrations/night-city.webp).
- Dimensions: 1536×1024; approximately 283 KB.
- Method: built-in image generation tool through the `imagegen` skill, then WebP encoding at quality 88 with Sharp. No change to the composition was made during encoding.
- This is original AI-generated concept artwork, not a photo of the owner’s location, a screenshot of a project, or an environment copied from another studio.
- The city supplies baked detail/lighting for a lightweight image-based scene. It is not a fully modelled 3D game level. Rain, mist, depth projection, camera movement, and neon grading are implemented separately in Three.js.
- The hero poster, GPU texture, and concept-art panel share the same URL/cache entry. No raw multi-megabyte PNG is shipped.

## Final generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic night-city environment key art for an immersive anime-game-style software engineer portfolio. Create an exceptionally high-quality ORIGINAL stylized 3D anime game environment render, LANDSCAPE 3:2. Scene: a rain-damp Japanese-inspired city at night, seen from a slightly elevated rooftop/street vantage looking along a beautifully composed avenue receding deep between dense layered mid-rise buildings toward far futuristic towers. Richly detailed architectural facades, roof antennae, overhead cables, small illuminated shopfronts and awnings, HVAC equipment, glowing windows, tiny distant taxis, street markings and puddles with convincing wet reflections. The mood is atmospheric, moody, quiet and cinematic with exquisite game-environment-level surface detail and convincing three-dimensional spatial depth. Deep ink navy/charcoal architecture, restrained electric cyan shop lights, warm amber windows and a handful of vermilion-red signs; dark blue haze and soft pale-blue moonlight. Detailed but beautifully art-directed and readable. Sophisticated anime background art meets a premium stylized 3D adventure game: strong clean silhouettes, delicate ink-like edges, painterly tactile materials, realistic soft bounced neon lighting, high-quality atmospheric perspective, cinematic composition. NOT low-poly, NOT flat vector, NOT a wireframe. Composition: principal rich city street vista in CENTER and RIGHT TWO THIRDS; LEFT THIRD contains dark quieter facade and dark sky providing room for later white HTML headlines, without being blank. Foreground parapet/roof elements near bottom right for depth. Show city from edge to edge. No people, no characters, no robots, no computer desk, no circuitry, no islands, no shrines, no flowers, no foliage-dominated scenery. No typography, no legible words on signs, no logos, no watermark, no webpage UI, no borders. Mesmerizing, extremely polished night city graphics; aim for the quality of a cinematic environment screenshot from an outstanding original anime game.
