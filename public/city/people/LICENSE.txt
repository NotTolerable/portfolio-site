CITY CIVILIAN CHARACTER ASSETS — CC0 1.0 Universal

Sam, Harper and Maya are fictional adult character GLBs from the Innerscene
Free Parts Library, adapted from MakeHuman / MPFB CC0 graphical assets.
The individual library pages dedicate the supplied files, including character
styling, materials and original authored animation, to the public domain (CC0).
The motions use Innerscene's authored mathematical IK recipes, not mocap data.
