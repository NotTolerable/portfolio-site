Derivatives: grounded and centred in metres, static geometry joined by material,
Meshopt compression, source textures converted to WebP at up to 1024 square.
The trash can includes the intact source variant and its leaning lid; the separate
rusty display duplicate is omitted. No source geometry decimation is applied.
The bicycle uses material factors and has no source texture images.
